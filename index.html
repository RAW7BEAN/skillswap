FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know

Is SkillSwap completely free to use? ▼
Yes! SkillSwap operates on a knowledge exchange model. You earn Skill Credits by teaching what you know and spend them to learn from others. No subscriptions or hidden fees.
What if I am only a beginner in my skill? ▼
Everyone has something to offer! Even teaching foundational basics, assignment walkthroughs, or conversational language practice is immensely valuable to beginners.
What is a Skill Passport? ▼
Your Skill Passport is a verifiable profile tracking your completed swap sessions, peer ratings, and earned skill mastery badges which you can share on resumes and LinkedIn.
